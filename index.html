About

R. B. Claymore has lived and worked across the United States, Europe, and Asia. That international experience shapes his fiction, which explores intelligence services, power, and the hidden mechanisms that shape the modern world.

His novels delve into ambition, secrecy, and the moral cost of knowing the truth, following characters caught between violence, loyalty, and survival in places where every decision leaves an irreversible mark.

Just a writer. Thrillers, intelligence, power, secrets and the dark side of truth.
